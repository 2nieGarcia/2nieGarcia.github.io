Hi, I'm Antonio. I build AI things, manage the chaos around them, and occasionally nap on hackathon couches.

## How it started

Games are how I got into coding. I was hooked on them, then a friend made one, and I thought that was the coolest thing a person could do. Turns out I like coding and solving puzzles even more than playing. I still haven't shipped a game of my own. It's on the list, right under "sleep".

## The turnaround

For a while I was coasting: no plan, no drive. At some point I decided to go all in once and see what happens. So far, so good.

That's what "what stands in the way becomes the way" means to me: the stuff that knocked me down turned into fuel. Marcus Aurelius also said "think of yourself as dead, now take what's left and live it properly." Dark? A little. Useful? Very.

## How I work

I started on the backend because I like logic. These days I also design the architecture and run the project side. If I don't know something yet, give me a night. My rule: if I can't count on anyone, I'll be the one people can count on.

I build for problems people around me actually have, which is why my projects are about healthcare, legal contracts, and electricity bills instead of another to-do app.

## The 50/50 rule

Half of me is trying to be the best version of myself. The other half is actually living. Why not both?

## The chain

Somebody inspired me to start building. A few people have told me they stepped out of their comfort zone because of me, and now they're inspiring someone else. That's the whole goal: keep the chain going.

## Why the angry angel

That's Alexandre Cabanel's The Fallen Angel, from 1847. Past me had that exact face. Current me is working on it.
