I've led teams across several hackathons. I design the system, assign the modules, and make sure everything actually comes together under pressure.

At InnOlympics 2026 I led the system design and backend orchestration for Nura, a 4-person team that shipped a multilingual healthcare navigator in 24 hours.

On Bantay-Dagitab, I held the system together with four JSON Schema contracts that every module had to conform to. That let the teams build in parallel without breaking each other.

I don't always know all the answers, but I've gotten good at making decisions fast for my team and figuring out the rest as we go.

My hackathon record: MERALCO IDOL Top 14 finalist with JOYBREAD, Gen AI Hackathon semi-finalist with JuryBee, plus the Alibaba Hackathon, HACK4GOV, Trend Micro, and DLSU Hacker Cup 2025.
